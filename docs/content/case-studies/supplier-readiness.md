## Context

A buyer or program wants to identify suppliers who can address a defined readiness need.

## Problem

General directories do not show whether a supplier is prepared for this specific opportunity.

## Intent

Find a qualified supplier for a concrete readiness challenge.

## Proposed Collaboration

Exchange a concise brief, confirm reciprocal interest, and schedule an introduction.

## Evidence

Unresolved. No pilot evidence is claimed.

## Constraints

Confidential business information, timing, eligibility, and capacity.

## Airlock

Release identities only after both parties approve the collision brief.

## Not-Yet Plan

Do not automate eligibility or readiness scoring before a pilot defines the criteria.

## Outcome

Pilot target: a useful introduction and a recorded next step.

## What Remains Unproven

Whether the workflow improves qualification or follow-through.
