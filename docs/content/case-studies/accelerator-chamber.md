## Context

A member network wants to turn broad requests into purposeful peer or partner introductions.

## Problem

Members have relevant capabilities but lack a structured way to state what they need now.

## Intent

Find a specific partner, resource, or introduction for a defined opportunity.

## Proposed Collaboration

Capture intent, prepare a collision brief, and use reciprocal consent before introduction.

## Evidence

Unresolved. This is not an observed case.

## Constraints

Member trust, privacy, staff capacity, and uneven request quality.

## Airlock

Keep identity hidden until both parties accept the proposed reason to meet.

## Not-Yet Plan

Defer broad network ranking and automated outreach.

## Outcome

Pilot target: a qualified introduction with a clear next action.

## What Remains Unproven

Whether structured intent increases member value.
