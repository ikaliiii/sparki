## Case Record Template

- **Status:** OBSERVED, ILLUSTRATIVE, or IN PROGRESS
- **Context:** What setting and participants are involved?
- **Problem:** What concrete need exists?
- **Intent:** What does the participant want to accomplish?
- **Proposed Collaboration:** What was the collision and why was it relevant?
- **Evidence:** What source supports each claim?
- **Constraints:** What limits disclosure or action?
- **Airlock:** What was gated until reciprocal consent?
- **Outcome:** What happened at Day 7 and Day 21?
- **What Remains Unproven:** What should not be inferred?

Never present an ILLUSTRATIVE case as a completed customer outcome.
