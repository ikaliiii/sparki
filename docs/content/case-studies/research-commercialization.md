title: "Research Commercialization"
---

**Status:** Illustrative

## Context

A research organization needs to find external partners for a specific translation opportunity.

## Problem

Potential partners may be discoverable but not ready, relevant, or mutually interested.

## Intent

Identify a partner for a defined commercialization or validation step.

## Proposed Collaboration

Translate the need into an intent card, assess a collision, and release identity after consent.

## Evidence

Unresolved. No research outcome is claimed.

## Constraints

IP sensitivity, technical fit, disclosure boundaries, and long timelines.

## Airlock

Keep sensitive details and identity gated until the parties agree to proceed.

## Not-Yet Plan

Do not publish patent-sensitive or attorney-eyes-only material.

## Outcome

Pilot target: a scoped conversation with a documented next step.

## What Remains Unproven

Whether the process shortens partner discovery without increasing disclosure risk.
