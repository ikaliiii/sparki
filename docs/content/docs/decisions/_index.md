title: "Decision Ledger"
---

A dated record of product and operating decisions. Use one of `BUILD`, `REVISE`, `DEFER`, or `INCONCLUSIVE` for status.

| Date | Question | Evidence | Decision | Status | Reason | Next Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-09-28 | What should ship first? | VERIFIED CONTEXT: a working handoff is needed. | Build the documentation and workflow foundation. | BUILD | It creates a source of truth for the next pilot. | Add source evidence and pilot materials. |
| 2026-09-28 | Should matching be fully automated now? | UNRESOLVED | Keep review manual or assisted. | DEFER | Criteria and outcome data are not established. | Define pilot thresholds. |

New entries must distinguish evidence from interpretation and record the next action.
