This section organizes the audiences and collaboration contexts that may benefit from an intent-first introduction.

## Primary Participant

A person or organization with a specific collaboration need and enough context to describe the desired next step.

## Primary Buyer

A network, program, chamber, accelerator, or operator that wants more useful introductions and better visibility into outcomes.
