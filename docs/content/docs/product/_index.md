## Current Product Thesis

Generic networking produces browsing and weak suggestions. Sparki is designed around a concrete reason for two parties to meet.

## Product Principles

- Intent over identity
- Evidence over assertion
- Action over browsing

## Core Workflow

**Intent Card** -> **Collision Brief** -> **Reciprocal Interest** -> **Airlock** -> **Consented Introduction** -> **Outcome Tracking**

## Workflow Components

### Intent Card

The participant states what they need, why it matters now, constraints, and the next action they want.

### Collision Brief

A concise explanation of why two intents may be relevant to each other, without prematurely exposing identity.

### Reciprocal Interest

Each side can express interest, request more information, or pass. A one-sided signal is not a match.

### Comprehension

Participants should understand the proposed reason to meet, what is known, and what will happen next.

### Airlock

An explicit consent boundary between a qualified collision and identity release.

### Consented Introduction

Identity and agreed context are released only after reciprocal approval.

### Outcome Tracking

The system records the defined next action and later outcome windows without turning activity into an unsupported success claim.

## Not-Yet Plan

Defer broad discovery, reputation scoring, opaque ranking, and unattended automation until evidence supports them.
