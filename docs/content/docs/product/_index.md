## What Sparki Is

Sparki is a system for turning a specific collaboration intent into a qualified, consented introduction and a measurable outcome.

## Why It Exists

Generic networking produces browsing and weak suggestions. Sparki is designed around a concrete reason for two parties to meet.

## Product Principles

- Intent over identity
- Evidence over assertion
- Action over browsing

## Core Workflow

**Intent Card** -> **Collision Brief** -> **Reciprocal Interest** -> **Airlock** -> **Consented Introduction** -> **Outcome**
